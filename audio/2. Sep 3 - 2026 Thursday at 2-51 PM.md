## September 3 Thursday

On Thursday the teacher explained how to factorize quadratic expressions:
***ax^2 + bx + c = 0***

Find two numbers which when added multiply to *c* and add up to *b*.

It should look like:
***(x+s)(x+t)***

Next, the teacher explained how to graph logarithmic functions.
Key point to remember:
***A Logarithmic Function is a vertical Asymptote, meaning it will never touch the y axis!***

Finally, the teacher reviewed the Trigonometric functions Sine, Cosine, Tangent, and the reciprocals: Cosecant, Secant, and Cotangent.

The teacher explained that the phrase "**ALL STUDENTS TAKE CALCULUS**" helps to remember that:

* In the first quadrant, all trig functions are positive,
* In the second quadrant, only Sine is positive,
* In the third quadrant, only tangent is positive,
* In the fourth quadrant, only Cosine is positive.

The teacher also explained a few trigonometric identities based on the Pythagorean Identity.

At the end of the class the students completed a Quiz on Precalculus concepts.
