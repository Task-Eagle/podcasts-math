Calculating percentages mentally becomes much faster when you use number properties instead of traditional multiplication. By treating 10% and 1% as building blocks or flipping the numbers, you can solve most percentage problems in seconds.
Here are the best mental math tricks to bypass slow decimal multiplication:
## 🔄 The Flip Rule ($x\%$ of $y = y\%$ of $x$)
Percentages are reversible. If finding a percentage of a number feels difficult, swap the two numbers to see if the reverse is easier.

* How it works: 12% of 50 sounds tough, but 50% of 12 is just half of 12, which is 6.
* Example: 64% of 25 is identical to 25% of 64 (one-quarter of 64), which is 16.

## 0️⃣ The "Drop the Zeros" Trick
When both the percentage and the number end in zero, you can cross out both zeros and multiply the remaining digits.

* How it works: This works because you are dividing both numbers by 10, which cancels out the division by 100 in a percentage.
* Example: 30% of 60 becomes 3 × 6 = 18.
* Example: 20% of 50 becomes 2 × 5 = 10.

## 🧱 The Building Blocks (10% and 1%)
You can calculate almost any irregular percentage (like 15% or 12%) by breaking it down into 10% and 1% pieces.

* Finding 10%: Move the decimal point one space to the left (e.g., 10% of 80 is 8).
* Finding 1%: Move the decimal point two spaces to the left (e.g., 1% of 80 is 0.8).

Once you have these blocks, you can easily combine them:

| To Find... | The Mental Trick | Example: Percentage of 80 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15% | Find 10%, then add half of that amount (5%). | 8 (which is 10%) + 4 (which is 5%) | 12 |
| 12% | Find 10%, then add 1% twice. | 8 (which is 10%) + 0.8 + 0.8 | 9.6 |
| 9% | Find 10%, then subtract 1%. | 8 (which is 10%) - 0.8 | 7.2 |
| 55% | Find 50% (half), then add 5% (one-tenth of the half). | 40 (which is 50%) + 4 (which is 5%) | 44 |
