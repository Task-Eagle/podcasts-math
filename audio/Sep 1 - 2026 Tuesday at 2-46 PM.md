## September 1

On Tuesday the teacher went through the syllabus and made sure that students understood how to correctly use AI:
Use AI as long as YOU completed the problem, not AI for you. You can check your answers with AI.

The teacher went through the most common types of functions:

**Quadratic, Cubic, Squaring functions,**
**Squaring, Logarithmic, Exponential, Trigonometric,**
**Absolute Value Functions, Piecewise Functions, Step Functions (of small integers)**

The teacher also explained about the Domain, Range, and Draw of functions.

Next, the Teacher spoke about the beauty of finding the inverse of functions.

Key takeaways:
*A linear function is straight* in the sense that it does not have curves.
*A quadratic function is a parabola*.

To find where a function intersects the *x* axis, you set y to zero and solve for *x*.
